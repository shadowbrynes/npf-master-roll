# Sample Personnel Self-Service Portal User Guide

## NPF EOD CBRN Personnel and Equipment Management System

### 1. Officer Sign-In & Dossier Access
- Log in using your assigned NPF email address and strong password.
- Access your individual **Officer Self-Service Dossier** showing your AP/F/NO, rank, assigned base, unit, duty post, and calculated statutory retirement date.

### 2. Gen.60 Document Review
- View your approved annual Gen.60 appraisal documents via secure 15-minute temporary signed links.

### 3. Submitting Correction Requests
- If any contact detail or record parameter is inaccurate, click **Request Correction**, select the field, enter proposed value, and upload supporting documents for admin approval.
