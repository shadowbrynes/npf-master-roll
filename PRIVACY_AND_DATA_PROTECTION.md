# Privacy & Data Protection Compliance

## NPF EOD CBRN Personnel and Equipment Management System

### 1. Data Minimization & Purpose Limitation
- Collects exclusively administrative, deployment, and statutory service data required for NPF EOD CBRN operations.
- No personal data is shared with external third-party tracking or advertising services.

### 2. Encryption Standards
- **Data at Rest**: AES-256 encryption on Supabase PostgreSQL storage volumes.
- **Data in Transit**: TLS 1.3 mandated across all web application and API endpoints.

### 3. Personnel Correction Rights
- Officers can submit formal correction requests via their Self-Service portal.
- Submissions require administrative approval before updating master database records.
